MT5 Expert Advisor development, with prop-firm rules built in

We code your trading rules into a MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor in MQL5, add guards for daily loss, maximum drawdown and lot size, and test it in the Strategy Tester and on demo before it touches a live or funded account.

What an Expert Advisor can and cannot do

An Expert Advisor (EA) is a program that runs inside MetaTrader 5 and trades by the rules written into it, on every tick, without hesitation. It does not make a losing strategy profitable, and it cannot know the future. We build and test the rules you give us, tell you plainly when a backtest looks unrealistic, and never promise returns. Forex and CFD trading carries a high risk of loss.

What we build in MQL5

  • Expert Advisors from your rules: indicator, price-action or session-based entries and exits, with fixed, percentage-risk or ATR-based position sizing.
  • Prop-firm guards: daily loss and maximum drawdown limits, lot and open-trade caps, trading-hour and news-window filters, and close-before-weekend rules, all set in the inputs.
  • Trade managers: break-even, trailing stops, partial closes and basket take-profit for trades you open by hand.
  • Conversions: TradingView Pine Script or MT4 (MQL4) strategies rewritten for MT5 and checked signal by signal. To keep the strategy in TradingView and trade it in MT5, see TradingView to MT5 webhook bridges. Trading futures on NinjaTrader instead? See NinjaTrader strategy development.
  • Custom indicators and dashboards: on-chart panels showing daily P&L, distance to your loss limits and the EA's state.
  • Alerts: Telegram or push notifications for entries, exits, errors and limit hits.

How an Expert Advisor with prop-firm guards works

  1. Market dataTicks and bars for each symbol and timeframe you trade
  2. SignalYour entry, exit and position-size rules
  3. Rule guardDaily loss, max drawdown, lot cap, hours and news filter
  4. ExecutionOrders with stop levels, slippage, requotes and retries handled
  5. Logs and alertsJournal, on-chart panel and Telegram messages

The rule guard checks equity on every tick. When a limit is close, the EA stops opening trades; when it is reached, it closes positions by your rule.

Prop-firm rules your EA should check

RuleWhat the EA doesDetail to confirm with your firm
Daily loss limitTracks equity from the day's start and stops before the limitMeasured from balance or equity, and when the day resets (server time)
Maximum drawdownStops trading and closes positions near the floorStatic from the starting balance, or trailing the highest balance or equity
Lot and exposure capsLimits lot size, open trades and total exposure per symbolAny maximum lot or margin rule in the firm's terms
News and weekend rulesPauses around listed news times and can close before the weekendWhich events count and how long the window is
Consistency rulesReports the share of profit from the best day so you can see itHow the firm calculates it; usually best handled by you, not forced by code

Rules differ between firms and change over time, so we put each limit in the EA's inputs rather than hard-coding one firm's numbers. Strategies a firm prohibits, such as latency arbitrage or copying trades across accounts, are not something we build around.

Testing before real money

  • MT5 Strategy Tester runs on real ticks where your broker provides them, with your spread and commission
  • An out-of-sample period the strategy was not tuned on, so the result is not just curve fitting
  • Forward testing on a demo account, compared trade by trade with the tester
  • Small live or funded-account start with the guards set tighter than the firm's limits

Tech stack

MQL5 (and MQL4 for MT4 work) with the standard trade classes; MT5 Strategy Tester and optimisation; WebRequest for Telegram alerts; Python with the MetaTrader5 package for research, data export or external dashboards; a Windows VPS or MetaQuotes VPS near your broker's server.

How we work

  1. Rules spec. You describe the strategy; we write it back as a precise spec, including the prop-firm limits you want enforced.
  2. Build and tester run. We code the EA and share Strategy Tester reports with your broker's data.
  3. Demo forward test. The EA runs on a demo account until its trades match the tester closely enough for you.
  4. Live start. You install it on your VPS and account; we help set the inputs.
  5. Handover. .mq5 source, .ex5 file, an inputs guide, and optional support for changes.

Risks and compliance

  • Leveraged forex and CFD trading can lose money quickly. Spreads widen, brokers requote and VPS connections drop; guards reduce the damage but cannot remove the risk.
  • You are responsible for your broker's and prop firm's terms and the laws where you live. Selling an EA or signals to others may need a licence in some countries.
  • Martingale and grid EAs can hide large drawdowns; if you want one, we build it with a hard loss cap and say plainly what the tester shows.
  • We do not build EAs meant to exploit platform or firm loopholes, such as latency arbitrage or hidden copy trading across accounts.

Questions clients ask

How much does an MT5 Expert Advisor cost?

At $50 per hour, a simple EA from clear rules is usually 30 to 60 hours ($1,500 to $3,000). An EA with prop-firm guards and a full settings panel is about 50 to 80 hours ($2,500 to $4,000), and converting a TradingView or MT4 strategy to MT5 is often 15 to 40 hours ($750 to $2,000).

Can you build an EA that passes a prop firm challenge?

Nobody can honestly guarantee that. We code your strategy and add guards so the EA stops before it breaks the firm's daily loss, drawdown or lot rules as you configure them. Whether the strategy reaches the profit target depends on the strategy and the market.

Are Expert Advisors allowed by prop firms?

Many firms allow EAs, but rules differ and change: some ban high-frequency trading, tick scalping, latency arbitrage, copy trading across accounts or third-party EAs. Check your firm's current rules before we start; we do not build EAs designed to get around them.

Can you convert my TradingView or MT4 strategy to MT5?

Yes. We rewrite Pine Script or MQL4 logic in MQL5 and compare signals bar by bar against the original on the same data, so you can see that the conversion matches.

Will I get the source code?

Yes. You receive the .mq5 source as well as the compiled .ex5 file, and the code is yours. We sign an NDA on request.

Does the EA run when my computer is off?

Only if it runs on a server. Most clients use a Windows VPS or the MetaQuotes built-in VPS close to their broker's server, usually around $10 to $30 a month.
