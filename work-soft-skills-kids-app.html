Soft skills app for children

An interactive app that helps children practise soft skills such as communication, empathy and teamwork through games and short activities.

The challenge

The client, based in Mexico, wanted children to practise everyday social skills such as communication, empathy and teamwork in a way that feels like play rather than a lesson. The app had to hold a child's attention, stay simple to use, and be safe for young users.

What we built

  • Interactive games and short activities built around specific soft skills
  • Child-friendly design with simple navigation and clear feedback
  • Activities that encourage reflection on feelings and other people's points of view
  • Content structured so new activities can be added over time
  • Support after delivery

How we approached it

We kept activities short and focused on one skill at a time, with immediate, encouraging feedback. After delivery the client wrote: “I appreciate your cooperation, professionalism, and commitment to providing support if needed in the future.”
