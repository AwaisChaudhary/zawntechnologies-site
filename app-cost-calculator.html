How the calculator works

Every software estimate is hours multiplied by a rate. The calculator adds up typical hour ranges for the platform you choose and each feature you tick, adjusts for design and integrations, then adds 20 percent for testing, project management and release. These are the same planning ranges we use in first conversations with clients at ZAWN Technologies, built from more than a decade of projects.

What makes an app cost more

  • Two native apps instead of one cross-platform app. Usually adds 30 to 40 percent.
  • Offline mode, chat and real-time features. Simple to describe, careful to build.
  • Integrations. Every outside system adds setup, error handling and testing.
  • AI and computer vision. Getting from a demo to dependable accuracy is where most AI hours go.

Read the full breakdown in what it costs to build an app with a remote team in 2026, or for AI projects, how much an AI chatbot costs to build.

Lower the cost without cutting quality

  • Launch with the one workflow your users need most; add the rest after real feedback.
  • Pick cross-platform over two native apps unless you need deep device features.
  • Use managed services for sign-in, payments and email.
  • Write down what version one will not do.

Questions about app costs

How accurate is this app cost calculator?

It gives a planning range based on the hours we use in first estimates at ZAWN Technologies. A real quote depends on your exact screens, integrations and quality targets, so treat the result as a starting point and ask us for a milestone-by-milestone estimate.

Why is the estimate a range?

The same feature can be simple or complex. A login screen with email only takes far less time than one with social sign-in, two-factor authentication and roles. The low end assumes simple versions; the high end assumes more complex ones.

Does the estimate include testing and project management?

Yes. The calculator adds 20 percent for testing, project management and release work, because those are part of every real project.

What hourly rate does the calculator use?

Our own rate of $50 per hour. You can use the hours figure to compare with other teams' rates.
