Sports analytics and motion analysis apps built from real match video

We turn phone or camera footage into numbers coaches and players can use: ball paths, shot speed, player movement and technique feedback.

Why teams hire us for this

Sports video is hard for computer vision. Balls are small and blurred, cameras shake, lighting changes court to court, and players block the view. Generic object detection models do not survive that. We have built ball tracking for padel and motion analysis for a client in the United Kingdom, so we know where these projects usually break and how to plan around it.

We decide early whether processing should run on the phone, on a laptop at the club or in the cloud, because that choice drives cost, speed and battery life more than any model choice.

What we build

  • Ball detection and tracking for padel, tennis, cricket, football and similar sports
  • Player pose and movement analysis for technique coaching
  • Shot, rally and event detection with highlight clips
  • Speed, distance and heatmap statistics from a single camera
  • Coach dashboards and athlete progress reports
  • Mobile apps that record, analyse and share sessions

How we work

Every engagement starts with a short discovery phase: we learn your users, your data and your constraints, then send a written plan with milestones and a fixed estimate per milestone. You see working software every one to two weeks, and the code lives in your repository from day one.

Our rate is $50 per hour. For fixed-scope work we quote per milestone so you know the cost before each stage starts.

Questions clients ask

Do we need special cameras?

Usually not. Most projects start with phone or action-camera footage. We test on your real videos first and tell you honestly if a fixed camera or higher frame rate would make a real difference.

Can it run on the phone?

Often yes, using compressed models with Core ML or TensorFlow Lite. Heavier analysis, such as full-match statistics, is usually faster and cheaper in the cloud.

How accurate will tracking be?

It depends on the sport, camera angle and video quality. We measure accuracy on a labelled sample of your footage and agree a target with you before building the full product.
