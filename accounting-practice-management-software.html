Practice management software for small accounting firms

Stop chasing clients for documents and tracking deadlines in spreadsheets. Request documents with automatic reminders, see every deadline in one view, and run each job from a checklist, at a price that suits a firm of one to ten people.

Get early access

The problem

In a small accounting or bookkeeping firm, much of the week goes on admin: emailing clients for missing documents, reminding them again, keeping deadlines in a spreadsheet and checking who is doing what. Full practice management suites are built for larger firms and take weeks to set up.

What it does

  • Document requests. Send a checklist of what you need; clients upload from a link and get automatic reminders until it is complete
  • Deadline tracking. Every filing and payroll deadline for every client in one calendar, with alerts before they are due
  • Job workflows. Repeatable checklists for month-end, year-end and tax returns, assigned to the right person
  • Client portal. Clients see what you need, upload securely and sign documents in one place
  • AI inbox helper. Sorts client emails by client and job, and drafts replies to routine questions for you to approve
  • Simple reporting. See work in progress, overdue items and which clients are holding you up

Questions

Who is this for?

Accounting, tax and bookkeeping firms with one to about ten people who want less admin without a complex, expensive system.

Does it replace our accounting software?

No. It sits alongside the ledger software you already use and handles the client, deadline and workflow side of the practice.

How do clients send documents?

Through a secure upload link or the client portal, no login needed for a one-off request. Reminders go out automatically until everything arrives.

Is client data secure?

Files are encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is limited to your team. We will share full security details with every early-access firm.
