MVP development that gets your idea in front of users quickly

We help founders cut an idea down to the version worth building first, then design, build and launch it in weeks with fixed prices per milestone.

Quick answer: we build MVPs at $50 per hour. A focused MVP with sign-in, 5 to 8 core screens and a simple backend is usually 250 to 450 hours ($12,500 to $22,500) and 6 to 12 weeks from agreed scope to launch. A scoping workshop and clickable prototype before that is usually 20 to 60 hours ($1,000 to $3,000, 1 to 2 weeks). You own the code, the app store and cloud accounts, and the designs from day one.

Why teams hire us for this

Most MVPs are too big. We work with you to find the one or two features that prove the idea, and leave the rest for after you have real users and feedback.

We pick the stack for your stage: Bubble or Flutter when speed matters most, React and Node when you expect to scale quickly. Either way, the code and accounts are yours from day one.

How we work

Every engagement starts with a short discovery phase: we learn your users, your data and your constraints, then send a written plan with milestones and a fixed estimate per milestone. You see working software every one to two weeks, and the code lives in your repository from day one.

  1. Scope (1 to 2 weeks): we write down the one workflow the MVP must prove, cut everything else to a "later" list and build a clickable prototype you can show to users or investors.
  2. Foundation (first 1 to 2 weeks of build): project setup, sign-in, database, deployment and the first screen running on your phone or a test link.
  3. Core features in milestones: the main workflow end to end first, then payments, notifications and the admin panel, with a test build at each milestone.
  4. Launch and learn: app store submission or production release, analytics checked, and a short list of improvements based on what the first users actually do.

Questions clients ask

How long does an MVP take?

Most of our focused MVPs take 6 to 12 weeks from agreed scope to launch. Add 1 to 2 weeks before that for scoping and a clickable prototype. MVPs with several user roles, payments and an admin panel usually take 3 to 5 months.

How much does an MVP cost?

At $50 per hour, a focused MVP is usually 250 to 450 hours, which is $12,500 to $22,500. We quote it as fixed-price milestones, so you know the cost of each stage before it starts. Our post on app development cost in 2026 breaks down realistic ranges.

Which features should go in the first version?

Only what is needed for one type of user to complete the main job your product promises, plus sign-in and a way to measure it. Settings pages, extra roles, social features and advanced admin tools usually wait. We write a "not in version one" list together, and it saves more money than any other document.

Should my MVP be no-code (Bubble) or custom code?

Bubble is a good fit for web marketplaces and internal tools where speed to launch matters most and the logic is standard. Custom code with Flutter, React or Node fits when you need a native mobile app, heavy data or AI work, or expect to scale quickly. We recommend one honestly after scoping; see Bubble.io vs custom code.

Mobile app or web app first?

Start where your users already are. If they need the camera, GPS, offline use or notifications, start with a cross-platform mobile app in Flutter or React Native. If they work at a desk or you need to test demand fast, a web app is cheaper and quicker to change.

Who owns the code and accounts?

You do, from day one. The code is in your Git repository, and the app runs in your own App Store, Google Play and cloud accounts, so you can continue with us or any other team.

Can you help after launch?

Yes. Most clients keep us on to improve the product based on what users actually do, billed by the hour on priorities you agree each week or month. See app maintenance and support.
