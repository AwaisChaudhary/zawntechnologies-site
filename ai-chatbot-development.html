Home / Services / AI chatbot development

AI chatbots that answer from your content and know when to hand over

We build chatbots for websites, WhatsApp and support tools that answer customer questions from your own documents, book or look up records, and pass hard cases to your team.

Why teams hire us for this

A chatbot that invents answers costs you customers. We ground every answer in your own help articles, policies and product data, show sources where it helps, and make the bot say so when it does not know.

We also build the parts that make a bot useful day to day: a dashboard of conversations, handover to a human agent, and a way for your team to update what the bot knows without calling a developer.

What we build

  • Website chat widgets trained on your help center and product pages
  • WhatsApp, Messenger and Telegram bots
  • Support assistants inside Zendesk, Intercom or your own help desk
  • Lead qualification and booking bots connected to your CRM or calendar
  • Voice and avatar assistants, including our Unity talking avatar work
  • Admin dashboards with conversation logs and answer quality checks

How we work

Every engagement starts with a short discovery phase: we learn your users, your data and your constraints, then send a written plan with milestones and a fixed estimate per milestone. You see working software every one to two weeks, and the code lives in your repository from day one.

Our rate is $50 per hour. For fixed-scope work we quote per milestone so you know the cost before each stage starts.

Questions clients ask

How long does a chatbot take?

A focused website or WhatsApp bot trained on your content usually takes 2 to 5 weeks, including testing with your real customer questions.

What does it cost to run?

Running costs depend on traffic and the model used. We estimate monthly cost before building and use caching and smaller models where they give the same quality.

Which AI model do you use?

We work with OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google and open-source models, and choose based on accuracy, privacy and cost for your case.
