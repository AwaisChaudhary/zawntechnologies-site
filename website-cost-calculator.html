What makes a website cost more

  • Content. Writing pages, sourcing photos and loading products takes longer than most people expect.
  • Custom design. A unique design for every page costs more than adapting a premium theme.
  • Things the site does. Booking, payments, member logins and integrations each add build and testing time.
  • Languages. Each language doubles the content work and adds layout checks.

Running costs after launch

Plan for hosting, your domain, any paid theme, plugin or app subscriptions, and someone to keep it updated and backed up. Shopify stores also pay Shopify's monthly plan and payment fees.

Questions about website costs

How much does a website cost in 2026?

At our rate of $50 an hour, a simple landing page is roughly $1,000 to $2,000, a 5 to 8 page business website about $2,500 to $4,500, a WordPress site with a blog about $4,000 to $8,000, and an online store $4,000 to $16,000 depending on whether it is Shopify or custom. Agencies in the US and UK usually charge more per hour, so compare the hours as well as the price.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page takes about a week. A business website takes 2 to 4 weeks. Online stores and portals take 4 to 12 weeks, mostly depending on how quickly content and products are ready.

Does the estimate include hosting and domain?

No. Hosting, domain names, paid themes, plugins and app subscriptions are separate running costs. For most small business sites they are a few hundred dollars a year or less.

What hourly rate does the calculator use?

Our own rate of $50 per hour, including 20 percent for testing, project management and launch. You can use the hours figure to compare with other teams' rates.
