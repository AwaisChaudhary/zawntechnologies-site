NinjaTrader strategy development

We code your futures trading rules as NinjaTrader 8 strategies, indicators and add-ons in NinjaScript (C#), with daily loss and drawdown guards, then test them in the Strategy Analyzer, on Market Replay and on a sim account before anything trades live.

What we build in NinjaTrader 8

  • Automated strategies for futures such as ES, NQ, CL and their micro contracts, from rules you define: entries, exits, stops, targets, sessions and position sizing.
  • Indicators drawn on the chart, with alerts, sounds or Telegram messages when a condition is met.
  • Trade managers and ATM helpers for manual traders: break-even, trailing stops, partial exits and one-click order tools.
  • Add-ons: custom windows, account monitors that flatten positions at a daily loss limit, and order logs exported for review.
  • Conversions from TradingView Pine Script, MT4/MT5 or Strategy Builder code into clean, readable NinjaScript.
  • TradingView to NinjaTrader bridges: a receiver for your alerts and a NinjaScript side that places the orders. See TradingView webhook bots.
  • Fixes and reviews of existing NinjaScript strategies that behave differently live than in the backtest.

A strategy follows the rules you give it and the limits you set. It does not find an edge on its own, and futures trading carries a real risk of loss.

How a NinjaTrader strategy works

  1. Market dataBars and ticks from your data feed or broker connection
  2. Strategy logicYour entry and exit rules in NinjaScript (C#)
  3. Risk guardsDaily loss, trailing drawdown, max contracts, session hours
  4. OrdersManaged orders or ATM strategies sent through your connection
  5. Logs and alertsTrades, errors and limit hits, with Telegram messages

Everything runs inside NinjaTrader 8 on a Windows PC or VPS. If the platform is closed or the connection drops, the strategy stops, so we add checks for that and tell you how to recover open positions.

The settings we check closely: Calculate mode (on bar close, on price change or on each tick), how the strategy starts when a position is already open, historical fill processing, and session templates, because these change results more than most traders expect.

Prop-firm guards

Many futures prop accounts run on NinjaTrader. We can build guards that stop new trades and flatten positions before a rule is broken: a daily loss limit, a trailing drawdown that follows your balance the way your firm measures it, a maximum number of contracts, no trading around set news times, and flat by a set time before the session closes. Firm rules differ and change often, including whether automated trading or a VPS is allowed at all, so you check your firm's current terms and we code the numbers you give us. For MetaTrader accounts, see MT5 Expert Advisor development.

  • Daily loss cap, maximum contracts and session hours that the strategy enforces itself
  • Clear handling of partial fills, rejected orders and reconnects
  • Optional Telegram alerts for entries, exits, errors and limit hits
  • Readable, commented source code that you own

Tech stack

NinjaTrader 8 and NinjaScript (C#, .NET); Strategy Analyzer, Market Replay and Sim accounts for testing; Visual Studio for debugging; the Automated Trading Interface or a NinjaScript add-on for external signals; Python (FastAPI) or Node.js for TradingView webhook receivers; Telegram Bot API for alerts; a Windows VPS for running strategies around the clock.

NinjaTrader, MT5 or a custom bot?

NinjaTrader suits futures traders who want charts, a built-in backtester and many futures brokers and prop firms. MT5 is the usual choice for forex and CFD brokers. A custom bot in Python on a broker API, such as Interactive Brokers, makes sense when you need several brokers, heavy data work or machine learning; see trading bot development. We will say which fits your strategy before you spend money.

How we work

  1. Rules spec. You describe the strategy; we write it back as a precise spec with every limit, so nothing is left to guesswork.
  2. Build and backtest. We code it and share Strategy Analyzer results with commission and slippage included.
  3. Replay and sim. The strategy runs on Market Replay, then on a sim account with live data, until its trades match your rules.
  4. Small live start. You run it on your account with small size and tight limits.
  5. Handover. Source code, a compiled export for import into NinjaTrader, a settings guide, and optional support.

Risks and compliance

  • Futures are leveraged. Automated trading can lose money quickly, and limits reduce damage but cannot remove risk. We never promise returns.
  • You are responsible for NinjaTrader's and your broker's terms, your prop firm's rules and the laws where you live. Selling strategies or signals, or trading other people's money, may need a licence.
  • We do not build strategies that copy trades across prop accounts against a firm's rules, hide automation where it is banned, or exploit data or platform errors.

Questions clients ask

How much does a NinjaTrader strategy cost to build?

At $50 per hour, an indicator or a small add-on is usually 15 to 35 hours ($750 to $1,750). An automated NinjaScript strategy from clear rules on one instrument is usually 30 to 60 hours ($1,500 to $3,000). A strategy with daily loss and trailing drawdown guards, a settings panel and Telegram alerts is about 50 to 90 hours ($2,500 to $4,500).

Is NinjaScript the same as C#?

NinjaScript is NinjaTrader's framework built on C#. Strategies, indicators and add-ons are C# classes that NinjaTrader 8 compiles and runs inside the desktop platform, so a strategy needs NinjaTrader 8 running on a Windows PC or VPS. The web and mobile apps do not run NinjaScript.

Can you convert my TradingView or MT5 strategy to NinjaTrader?

Yes. We rewrite the rules in NinjaScript and compare signals bar by bar with the original on the same data, because session times, bar building and order fills differ between platforms. If you would rather keep the strategy in TradingView, we can build a receiver that passes your alerts to NinjaTrader instead.

Can TradingView alerts place trades in NinjaTrader?

Not directly. TradingView sends alerts to a web address, and NinjaTrader 8 has no web address of its own. We build a small receiver on a server that checks each alert, plus a NinjaScript add-on or Automated Trading Interface link on the PC running NinjaTrader that places the order with your limits. Hosted relay services exist too, and we can tell you when one is enough.

Do prop firms allow automated trading on NinjaTrader?

It depends on the firm, and rules change often. Some futures prop firms allow automated strategies, some allow only bots you wrote yourself, some forbid a VPS, and some ban fully automated trading on funded accounts. Read your firm's current rules before we start; we build the guards, but following the firm's terms is your responsibility.

Will the strategy be profitable?

No developer can promise that. The strategy trades the rules you define, with the limits you set. Backtests in the Strategy Analyzer often look better than live trading because of slippage, fees and fills, so we test on Market Replay and a sim account before you trade real money. Futures are leveraged and losses can exceed what you expect.
