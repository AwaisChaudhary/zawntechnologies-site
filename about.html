An engineering team that has been shipping since 2014

ZAWN Technologies started in February 2014 and has worked with clients across Europe, North America, the Middle East and Asia. We are best suited to projects where technical complexity, reliability and long-term maintainability matter.

In short: ZAWN Technologies is a software engineering company (a software house in Lahore, Pakistan), founded in February 2014 by Awais Chaudhry. A team of about 25 engineers, designers and testers builds AI, computer vision, mobile, web, IoT and automation software for clients in Europe, North America, Australia, the Middle East and Asia. Over 356 client reviews on Freelancer.com average 4.9 out of 5, with 95% of projects on time and 99% on budget.

How we think about projects

We start with the business problem, not the technology. A clear understanding of who will use the software and what has to be true for it to succeed saves more money than any framework choice. Then we build the right solution and deliver it in small steps you can check: a written plan, milestones with a fixed price, a working demo every one to two weeks and the code in your own repository from day one.

What we have built

Twelve years of client work has given us unusually broad experience. A few examples of real projects:

  • AI and computer vision: a padel ball-tracking app, a sports motion analysis app for a UK client, an AI image generation app, LLM chatbots and document search, and an AI autodialer running on a Raspberry Pi.
  • Mobile and web products: a farm management app, a tree work estimation app, an interactive soft skills app for children for a client in Mexico, a Bubble.io marketplace for an Australian client and SaaS backends for startups.
  • Business systems: Salesforce configuration and CRM integration, Dynamics 365 Sales setup and training, a white-label Zoho Creator solution, Microsoft Business Central integration, a SharePoint intranet, a Power App for a construction workflow, and Odoo and VTiger integrations.
  • Hardware and embedded: a wearable sensor that monitors horses' heart rate, speed and recovery during training, firmware updates for a Zynq-7000 FPGA board, stepper motor control, label printer modules and Android kiosk panels.
  • Games and 3D: Unity games and training apps, a Unity-to-Unreal port and an animated AI business assistant with speech recognition and lip-synced replies.
  • Automation and trading: crypto arbitrage and algorithmic trading bots, MetaTrader expert advisors, Discord and Telegram bots, web scraping and workflow automation.

Leadership

Awais Chaudhry, Founder and CEO. Awais founded ZAWN Technologies in 2014 after studying Computer Science at Lancaster University in the UK and COMSATS Institute of Information Technology in Pakistan. He still leads technical planning on client projects: scoping, architecture and the milestone plan, so clients talk to someone who understands the engineering, not only the sales side.

The team

We are a remote-first team of about 25 people based in Lahore, Pakistan: mobile, web and backend developers, AI and computer vision engineers, Unity developers, embedded engineers, UI/UX designers and QA testers. Each project gets a lead engineer who stays with it from plan to launch. Our working hours overlap with the US East Coast and the UK, and we keep clients updated in writing so nothing depends on a call.

How we work with clients

  1. Understand the goal: users, budget, deadline and what success looks like.
  2. Written plan: milestones, each with a fixed price and a demo you can test.
  3. Build in small steps: working software every one to two weeks, in your own repository.
  4. Launch and support: release, handover and ongoing maintenance. Most clients keep working with us after launch.
