Voice AI that listens, understands and answers naturally

We build voice assistants, transcription tools and talking characters that combine speech recognition, language models and natural-sounding speech.

Why teams hire us for this

Voice products live or die on delay. Every step, from hearing the user to speaking the answer, adds waiting time, and anything over a second or two feels broken. We design the pipeline so each stage starts as early as possible.

Our team has built a Unity business assistant that speaks with customers, and our background includes audio and voice work, so we care about how the result actually sounds.

Hire an AI voice agent developer: Vapi, Retell or custom

Most voice agent requests are phone agents: an AI receptionist that answers calls, books appointments, qualifies leads or routes callers to a person. You can build these on a hosted voice platform such as Vapi or Retell, or as a custom pipeline. We do both and help you pick:

Hosted platform (Vapi, Retell)Custom pipeline
How it worksThe platform runs speech-to-text, the language model and the voice; we write the prompt, tools and integrationsWe connect telephony (for example Twilio), speech-to-text, the model and text-to-speech ourselves, or use a realtime speech model
Time to first working callDaysWeeks
Running costPlatform fee per minute plus model, voice and phone costsOnly the underlying services and your server, so often lower at high call volume
ControlLimited to what the platform exposesFull control of latency, data storage and behaviour
Best forProving the idea, low to medium volumeHigh volume, strict data rules, unusual call flows

Whichever route you choose, a useful voice agent needs the same work: a clear call script with what the agent must never say, tools that read and write your calendar, CRM or booking system, a hand-over to a human, call recordings and transcripts you can review, and tests with real recorded calls before launch. We also measure the delay between the caller finishing and the agent answering, because that decides whether calls feel natural.

For outbound calling, rules are strict: in the US the FCC ruled in February 2024 that AI-generated voices count as “artificial” voices under the TCPA, so automated outbound calls need the prior consent that law requires. You decide who is called and must follow the laws where you and the people you call are; we build consent records, opt-outs and calling-hour limits into outbound agents. Questions clients ask

Which languages are supported?

Modern speech models handle dozens of languages and accents. We test with recordings from your real users before committing.

Can it run offline?

Some speech recognition and synthesis can run on the device. We check accuracy and speed on your target hardware.

Can it use a custom voice?

Yes, with the speaker's consent and the right provider terms.

Should I build my voice agent on Vapi or Retell, or custom?

Start on a hosted platform such as Vapi or Retell when you want working calls in days and your volume is modest. Move to a custom pipeline when per-minute platform fees add up at high volume, or you need full control of data and latency. We can build on either and keep your prompts, tools and integrations portable.

How much does an AI voice agent cost to build?

At $50 per hour, an inbound AI receptionist on Vapi or Retell with calendar booking is usually 20 to 50 hours ($1,000 to $2,500). A voice agent connected to your CRM with human hand-over is usually 40 to 90 hours, and a custom voice pipeline with a dashboard 120 to 250 hours. Platform, phone and model usage are billed separately by the providers.

Can the voice agent make outbound calls?

Yes, but only with proper consent. In the US the FCC treats AI-generated voices as artificial voices under the TCPA, so automated outbound calls need prior consent. We add consent records, opt-out handling and calling-hour limits; you remain responsible for following the laws that apply to your calls.
