Stripe and payment integration that handles the edge cases

Checkout, subscriptions, marketplace payouts and invoicing with Stripe and PayPal, built so failed payments, refunds and disputes are handled properly.

Why teams hire us for this

Taking a payment is easy. The hard parts are failed renewals, proration, refunds, taxes, marketplace payouts and keeping your database in step with Stripe. Mistakes there cost money and support time.

We have integrated Stripe across web apps, mobile apps and no-code platforms, and we test every webhook path before launch.

What we build

  • One-off checkout and payment links
  • Subscriptions with trials, upgrades and proration
  • Marketplace payments and payouts with Stripe Connect
  • Invoices, receipts and customer billing portals
  • In-app purchases alongside web payments
  • Migration from another payment provider

How we work

Every engagement starts with a short discovery phase: we learn your users, your data and your constraints, then send a written plan with milestones and a fixed estimate per milestone. You see working software every one to two weeks, and the code lives in your repository from day one.

Our rate is $50 per hour. For fixed-scope work we quote per milestone so you know the cost before each stage starts.

Questions clients ask

How long does a Stripe integration take?

A standard checkout or subscription setup usually takes 1 to 3 weeks including testing. Marketplaces with payouts take longer.

Do you handle taxes and invoices?

Yes. We can set up Stripe Tax and invoicing, or connect to your accounting tool.

Can you fix our existing payment bugs?

Yes. We review webhooks, logs and your database to find mismatches, then fix and backfill the data.
