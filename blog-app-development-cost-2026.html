What does it cost to build an app with a remote team in 2026?

By Awais Chaudhry, 13 November 2026

Most people asking this question want a single number. The honest answer is a range, and the range depends on a handful of decisions you can make before talking to any developer. Here is how we estimate projects at ZAWN Technologies, using our own rate of $50 per hour.

Start with hours, not price

Price is hours times rate, so the useful question is how many hours your product needs. These are the planning ranges we use in first conversations. They are starting points, not quotes.

Type of productTypical hoursAt $50 per hour
Focused MVP: one platform or cross-platform, sign-in, 5 to 8 core screens, simple backend250 to 450$12,500 to $22,500
Business app: roles, admin panel, payments, notifications, offline use500 to 900$25,000 to $45,000
AI or computer vision product: model work, evaluation, app and backend900 to 1,600$45,000 to $80,000

What pushes the number up

  • Two native apps instead of one cross-platform app. Usually adds 30 to 40 percent.
  • Offline mode with sync. Simple to describe, careful to build, and easy to underestimate.
  • Integrations. Every outside system (accounting, CRM, hardware) adds testing and edge cases.
  • AI accuracy targets. Getting from a good demo to dependable results is where most AI hours go.
  • Unclear scope. The most expensive line item is building something twice.

What brings it down

  • Launch with the one workflow your users need most, and add the rest after real feedback.
  • Use managed services for sign-in, payments and email instead of building them.
  • Write down what the first version will not do. It saves more money than any other document.

Do not forget running costs

Budget for hosting, app store fees, third-party APIs (AI models are often the largest), and maintenance. A common rule is 15 to 20 percent of the build cost per year to keep an app current with new operating system versions and security updates.
