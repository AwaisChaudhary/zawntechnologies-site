WhatsApp AI chatbots that answer, book and sell for you

We build WhatsApp chatbots on the official WhatsApp Business Platform: an AI assistant trained on your business that answers customers 24/7, takes bookings and payments, checks orders and hands over to a person when it should.

Quick answer: we build AI WhatsApp chatbots at $50 per hour on the official WhatsApp Business Platform. A focused FAQ and lead bot is usually 8 to 20 hours ($400 to $1,000, about one week). An AI assistant that also books appointments, takes payments or checks orders is usually 30 to 80 hours ($1,500 to $4,000, 2 to 4 weeks). You own the code, the WhatsApp number and the data.

Why businesses put an AI assistant on WhatsApp

In much of Latin America, Europe, the Middle East, Africa and South Asia, customers would rather send a WhatsApp message than fill in a form or call. The problem is volume: the same twenty questions arrive all day, replies come late at night or not at all, and leads go cold.

A well-built WhatsApp assistant answers instantly, in the customer's language, using your real prices, policies and availability. It takes the routine work off your team and passes the conversations that need a person straight to them, with a summary.

What we build

  • AI customer support: answers from your FAQs, catalogue, documents and policies, with retrieval (RAG) so it does not invent answers
  • Bookings and appointments: clinics, salons, studios, consultants and restaurants, synced with Google Calendar, Calendly or your booking system, with reminders
  • Payments inside the chat: payment links with Stripe, Mercado Pago, Asaas (Pix) or your gateway, confirmed automatically by webhook
  • Order and delivery tracking: customers send an order or tracking number and get the live status from your system
  • Lead capture and qualification: asks the right questions, scores the lead and sends it to your CRM or sales team
  • Human handover: a shared inbox for your team, with the AI summary of the conversation so nobody starts from zero
  • Broadcasts and follow-ups: approved template messages for reminders, abandoned carts and re-engagement, only to customers who opted in

Official API, not risky shortcuts

Many cheap WhatsApp bots automate the normal WhatsApp app through unofficial libraries. They work until Meta notices, and then the number is blocked, sometimes with years of customer history on it. We build on the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) through Meta, with a verified business, approved message templates and proper opt-in, so your number is safe and can grow to high volumes.

What you get

  • The bot running on your own WhatsApp Business number and your own Meta account
  • Source code in your Git repository and hosting in your own cloud account
  • An admin page to edit answers, documents and business information without a developer
  • Conversation logs and simple reports: questions answered, handovers, bookings, payments
  • Tests with your real customer questions before launch, in every language you serve
  • A short handover call and written guide for your team

How we work

  1. Discovery (1 to 3 days): we collect your top customer questions, the systems the bot must use and the moments it should hand over to a person.
  2. WhatsApp setup: Meta business verification, the WhatsApp Business number on the Cloud API and the message templates you need.
  3. Build and train: the AI assistant, its knowledge base and integrations, delivered in milestones you can test on your own phone.
  4. Launch and tune: go live, read real conversations for the first week and improve the answers and handover rules.

Questions clients ask

How much does a WhatsApp chatbot cost?

A focused FAQ and lead-capture bot is usually 8 to 20 hours ($400 to $1,000). An AI assistant with bookings or order lookups is usually 30 to 80 hours ($1,500 to $4,000). A multi-agent support system with CRM or helpdesk integration is usually 100 to 250 hours ($5,000 to $12,500). Meta's own messaging fees are separate and paid by you directly.

Will my number get banned?

Not when it runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API), which is what we use. Unofficial tools that automate the normal WhatsApp app break Meta's terms and are what usually gets numbers blocked. We also set up opt-in, message templates and sending limits the way Meta expects.

Can the bot answer from our own information without making things up?

Yes. The AI answers from your documents, catalogue, prices and policies using retrieval (RAG), and we add rules so it says it does not know and hands over to a person instead of guessing. Prices, stock and order status come live from your systems, not from the AI's memory.

Can it work in Portuguese, Spanish, Arabic or Urdu?

Yes. Modern language models handle most languages well, and the bot replies in the customer's language. We test it with real questions in each language you need before launch.

What does it cost to run each month?

Two parts: Meta's per-message fees, where replies inside the 24-hour customer service window are currently free and business-started template messages are charged by country and category, and the AI model usage, often a few cents per conversation. We estimate both for your volume before we build.

Can it connect to our CRM, store or booking system?

Yes. We connect to your systems through their APIs: for example HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, Freshdesk, Shopify, WooCommerce, Google Calendar, Calendly, Stripe, Mercado Pago or your own database, so the bot can create leads, book slots, take payments and check orders.
