HOA management software built for volunteer boards

Collect dues online, chase late payments automatically, message every owner and keep documents in one place, without a property manager or a spreadsheet. Built for self-managed HOAs and small condo associations.

Get early access

The problem

Most small HOAs are run by volunteers with day jobs. Dues are tracked in spreadsheets, reminders go out by hand, owners ask the same questions by email, and the treasurer spends evenings reconciling payments. The tools built for property managers are priced and designed for professionals, and reviewers of the simpler ones still mention a learning curve, costly add-ons and clunky messaging.

What it does

  • Online dues. Owners pay by card or bank transfer from a simple link; payments match to the right home automatically
  • Automatic reminders and late fees. Polite reminders before and after the due date, with late fees applied the way your rules say
  • Owner messages. Email and text announcements to every owner, or just one street or building
  • Documents and minutes. Bylaws, rules, budgets and meeting minutes in one place, with an owner portal
  • AI-drafted letters. Violation notices, welcome letters and meeting notices drafted for you to review and send
  • Treasurer reports. Income, overdue accounts and a budget-vs-actual report your board can read in two minutes

Questions

Who is this for?

Self-managed HOAs, condo and townhome associations, and small community associations run by volunteer boards, typically 10 to 300 homes.

Do owners need to install anything?

No. Owners pay and read announcements from a link in their email or text, and can log in to a simple owner portal from any browser.

How do payments work?

Owners pay by card or bank transfer through a secure payment processor, and the money goes straight to the association's bank account. We never hold your funds.

Can we import our current owner list and balances?

Yes. Upload a spreadsheet of owners, homes and balances and we set it up for you during onboarding.

When can we start?
