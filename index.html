Process

How a project runs

The same four stages for a two-week fix or a year-long product.

  1. Understand the problem

    A call and a short written brief. We ask about users, constraints and what success looks like before we talk about technology.

  2. Scope and estimate

    A plan broken into milestones, each with hours, cost and a demo you can check.

  3. Build in short cycles

    Working software every one to two weeks, in your repository, with progress you can test yourself.

  4. Launch and support

    Release, monitoring and a handover. Most clients keep us on for updates after launch.
