Custom business software that fits how your company actually works

When spreadsheets and off-the-shelf tools stop fitting, we build ERP, CRM, inventory and operations software around your real processes.

Why teams hire us for this

Generic software forces your team to change how it works. Custom software should do the opposite. We spend time with the people who will use it, map the current process, and build the smallest system that removes the real bottlenecks.

We integrate with accounting, payment and communication tools you already use, and deliver in milestones so your team can start using parts of the system early.

What we build

  • ERP and inventory systems
  • CRMs and sales pipelines
  • Booking, scheduling and dispatch tools
  • Quoting and estimating software, like our tree work estimation app
  • Client and supplier portals
  • Reporting dashboards across your data

How we work

Every engagement starts with a short discovery phase: we learn your users, your data and your constraints, then send a written plan with milestones and a fixed estimate per milestone. You see working software every one to two weeks, and the code lives in your repository from day one.

Our rate is $50 per hour. For fixed-scope work we quote per milestone so you know the cost before each stage starts.

Questions clients ask

How much does custom business software cost?

It depends on scope. A focused internal tool can start around a few thousand US dollars; larger systems are quoted per milestone after a discovery phase.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. We build responsive web apps by default, and native mobile apps when your team works in the field or offline.

Who owns the code?

You do. The code lives in your repository from day one.
