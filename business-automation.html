Automation tools that take repetitive work off your team

If your team copies data between systems, builds the same report every week or processes documents by hand, we can build a tool that does it reliably.

Why teams hire us for this

Off-the-shelf automation platforms are great until a workflow needs custom logic, high volume or a system without an integration. That is where we come in, with scripts, services and small internal apps built around the way your business actually works.

We add AI only where it earns its place, such as reading invoices or classifying emails, and keep a human review step wherever a mistake would be expensive. We recently delivered an automation tool for a client in the United States as a fixed-price project.

What we build

  • Data sync between CRMs, spreadsheets, accounting and e-commerce tools
  • Automated reports and dashboards delivered by email or Slack
  • Document and invoice processing with AI extraction
  • Web data collection and monitoring tools
  • Order, inventory and fulfilment workflows
  • Internal admin tools that replace spreadsheets

How we work

Every engagement starts with a short discovery phase: we learn your users, your data and your constraints, then send a written plan with milestones and a fixed estimate per milestone. You see working software every one to two weeks, and the code lives in your repository from day one.

Our rate is $50 per hour. For fixed-scope work we quote per milestone so you know the cost before each stage starts.

Questions clients ask

Can you work with our existing software?

Yes. If a system has an API, export or even a stable web interface, we can usually connect to it. We check access and limits before quoting.

Should we use Zapier or custom code?

If a no-code tool covers the job, we will tell you. Custom code makes sense for high volume, complex rules, or when monthly platform fees add up.

What happens when something fails?

We build in logging, retries and alerts so your team knows when a step fails and nothing silently disappears.
