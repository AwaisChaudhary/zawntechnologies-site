Shopify stores, themes and apps built for speed and sales

We build custom Shopify themes, private and public Shopify apps, and integrations with your ERP, inventory and marketing tools.

Why teams hire us for this

Many Shopify stores slow down under a pile of apps doing small jobs. We replace them with lean theme code and custom functionality where it makes sense, which usually improves speed and lowers monthly app fees.

When your store needs to talk to other systems, such as inventory, accounting or a warehouse, we build the integration and keep orders and stock in sync.

What we build

  • Custom Shopify themes and Online Store 2.0 sections
  • Private and public Shopify apps
  • Checkout extensions and Shopify Functions
  • ERP, inventory, shipping and accounting integrations
  • Migrations from WooCommerce, Magento and other platforms
  • Speed optimisation and app clean-up

How we work

Every engagement starts with a short discovery phase: we learn your users, your data and your constraints, then send a written plan with milestones and a fixed estimate per milestone. You see working software every one to two weeks, and the code lives in your repository from day one.

Our rate is $50 per hour. For fixed-scope work we quote per milestone so you know the cost before each stage starts.

Hire Shopify developers in Pakistan

Our Shopify developers work from our office in Lahore, Pakistan, on Liquid themes, Online Store 2.0 sections, custom apps and integrations. Because the same team also builds APIs and back-end systems, we can connect your store to ERP, inventory or marketplace systems without a separate integration vendor.

Typical Shopify jobs and planning ranges at our rate of $50 per hour:

  • Theme fixes, speed work or removing unneeded apps: 10 to 40 hours ($500 to $2,000)
  • Custom theme or major redesign from your design files: 80 to 200 hours ($4,000 to $10,000)
  • Custom private Shopify app or ERP and inventory integration: 60 to 200 hours ($3,000 to $10,000)

Questions clients ask

Can you work on our existing theme?

Yes. We review the theme first and fix or extend it rather than rebuilding unless a rebuild is clearly cheaper.

Do you build Shopify apps for the App Store?

Yes, both private apps for one store and public apps for the Shopify App Store.

Can you move our store from WooCommerce?

Yes, including products, customers, orders and SEO redirects so you keep your search rankings.

How much does it cost to hire a Shopify developer in Pakistan?

Rates in Pakistan vary widely with experience. ZAWN charges $50 per hour with fixed-price milestones. Small theme and speed fixes are typically 10 to 40 hours; a custom theme is typically 80 to 200 hours.

Do you need full access to our Shopify store?

No. We work as a collaborator account with only the permissions the task needs, and build theme changes on an unpublished copy so your live store is not affected until you approve.
