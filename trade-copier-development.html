Trade copier and copy trading bot development

We build trade copiers that copy orders from one master account or signal source to your other accounts: MT4 and MT5 copiers, cross-platform copiers to cTrader or NinjaTrader, crypto account copiers for Binance, Bybit and OKX, and Telegram signal copiers, with symbol mapping, lot sizing and loss limits on every follower.

What we build

  • MT4 and MT5 local copiers: a master Expert Advisor and follower EAs on the same PC or VPS, copying opens, closes, partial closes and stop or target changes between terminals, even at different brokers.
  • Remote copiers: the master sends each trade to a small server, and followers on other machines or brokers pick it up within a second or two, with a log of every copy.
  • Cross-platform copiers: MT5 to cTrader through the cTrader Open API, MT5 or TradingView to NinjaTrader 8, or one signal fanned out to several brokers.
  • Crypto account copiers between your own Binance, Bybit, OKX or Kraken accounts and sub-accounts through their official APIs, with position checks after every copy.
  • Telegram and Discord signal copiers that read signal messages ("BUY XAUUSD 2350, SL 2340, TP 2370"), turn them into orders on MT5 or an exchange, and follow later edits such as "move SL to entry" or "close half".
  • Copy trading platforms where followers connect their own accounts to a leader, with a web dashboard, per-follower settings and billing. These raise legal questions, covered below.
  • Fixes for existing copiers that miss trades, open duplicates or copy the wrong size.

A copier repeats trades; it does not judge them. If the master or signal loses, the followers lose too, scaled by the settings you choose.

How a trade copier works

  1. SourceMaster account, exchange account or Telegram signal channel
  2. ListenerMaster EA, exchange WebSocket or message reader that spots each trade
  3. Copier rulesSymbol mapping, lot sizing, filters and per-account risk limits
  4. FollowersOrders placed on each follower account, then checked against the master
  5. Logs and alertsEvery copy, delay, skip and error, with Telegram messages

The copier keeps a map of which follower order belongs to which master order. That map is what lets it close, modify and recover trades correctly after a restart, and it is the part cheap copiers most often get wrong.

Lot sizing: how followers size each trade

MethodHow it worksWhen it fits
Fixed lotEvery follower trade uses the same size, such as 0.10 lotsTesting, or followers with similar balances
MultiplierFollower size = master size × a factor, such as 0.5 or 2Your own accounts at different sizes
Balance or equity ratioSize scales with follower equity divided by master equityAccounts of different sizes that should carry the same risk
Risk per tradeSize set so the stop loss risks a set percentage of the follower's equitySignals that always include a stop loss

Every method then rounds to the broker's minimum lot and lot step, and a trade that would break a follower's limit is skipped and logged rather than forced through.

Problems a good copier handles

  • Symbol names differ: EURUSD, EURUSD.m and EURUSDpro, or XAUUSD and GOLD, plus different contract sizes and digits for indices and metals.
  • Delay and slippage: followers fill later and at a different price. We record the delay and price difference of every copy and can skip a trade if price has moved too far.
  • Hedging and netting accounts in MT5, where one copies positions and the other copies net exposure.
  • Partial closes and modifications: closing a third, moving a stop to break-even or changing a target must reach every follower exactly once.
  • Reconnects and restarts: after a lost connection, the copier compares master and follower positions and fixes the difference instead of opening duplicates.
  • Signal text that changes: channel admins change their message format. Our parsers flag messages they cannot read instead of guessing.

Telegram signal copiers

There are two ways to read a Telegram channel. If you run the channel, a bot built on the Telegram Bot API can be added as an admin and receives each post. If you only follow a channel, a bot cannot read it; the copier has to sign in as your own Telegram user account through the Telegram API, which you set up with your own API ID and which must follow Telegram's terms. We parse messages with fixed rules first. A language model can read messy formats, but we only let it prepare an order that the rules then check, and new setups can run in confirm mode, where you tap to approve each trade. See Telegram and Discord bot development for other Telegram bots.

Prop firms, brokers and the law

Many prop firms ban copying trades from other people, copying the same trades across accounts held by different people, or copying between firms, and some allow copying only between your own accounts at the same firm. Brokers and exchanges have their own terms for API use. Selling signals, running a copy trading service for others or trading other people's money may need a licence or registration where you or your followers live. Check these before we start; we can build the limits, but the rules and the licences are your responsibility. Exchanges such as Binance and Bybit also run their own copy trading programs, which may suit a public leader better than a custom platform.

Features we include as standard

  • Per-follower settings: lot method, maximum lot, symbols allowed, trading hours and reverse mode on or off
  • Per-follower daily loss limit and maximum open trades, with a kill switch
  • Position reconciliation after restarts, so no trade is missed or doubled
  • A copy log with delay and price difference for each trade
  • Optional Telegram alerts for copies, skips, errors and limit hits
  • Readable, commented source code that you own

Tech stack

MQL4 and MQL5 for MetaTrader copiers; C# for cTrader Open API and NinjaScript; Python (FastAPI, asyncio) or Node.js for the copy server and exchange copiers, using official exchange REST and WebSocket APIs; Telethon or the Telegram Bot API for signal reading; PostgreSQL or SQLite for the order map and logs; Redis for fast hand-off between master and followers; a Windows VPS for MetaTrader and NinjaTrader, Linux for crypto copiers.

Custom copier or ready-made software?

Ready-made copiers for MT4 and MT5 are cheap and work well when you copy between a few of your own accounts on one VPS. A custom copier makes sense when you need a platform the tools do not support, a signal format they cannot read, rules of your own (filters, risk per trade, time windows), or a service with many followers and a dashboard. We will say if an existing tool is enough before you pay for a build.

How we work

  1. Copy spec. We list the source, every follower, symbol names, the lot method and every limit, and agree what happens in each failure case.
  2. Build on demo accounts. The copier runs between demo accounts until opens, partial closes, modifications and restarts all copy correctly.
  3. Delay test. We measure copy delay and price difference on your VPS and share the log.
  4. Small live start. You run it live with small size and tight limits, raising them yourself.
  5. Handover. Source code, compiled files, a settings guide and optional support.

Risks and compliance

  • Copying multiplies exposure: one bad master trade hits every follower. Limits reduce damage but cannot remove risk, and we never promise returns.
  • You are responsible for your broker's, exchange's, platform's and prop firm's terms and the laws where you and any followers live.
  • We do not build copiers meant to break prop-firm rules, hide copying where it is banned, exploit latency or price-feed errors. We do not build betting bots.

Questions clients ask

How much does a trade copier cost to build?

At $50 per hour, an MT4 or MT5 copier from one master to several accounts on one VPS is usually 25 to 50 hours ($1,250 to $2,500). A Telegram signal copier to MT5 or one exchange is about 40 to 80 hours ($2,000 to $4,000), and a remote or cross-platform copier with a copy server is about 60 to 120 hours ($3,000 to $6,000). A copy trading platform with followers, a dashboard and billing starts around $12,500.

Can you copy trades between different brokers or platforms?

Yes. Between MT4 and MT5 terminals at different brokers we map symbol names, contract sizes and lot steps for each account. Between platforms, such as MT5 to cTrader or MT5 to NinjaTrader, the master sends each trade to a small server and the follower side places it through that platform's own interface, such as the cTrader Open API or a NinjaScript add-on.

How fast is a trade copier?

On one VPS, a local MT4 or MT5 copier usually copies in well under a second. A remote copier adds network time, often a second or two. Followers will still fill at slightly different prices than the master, so we log the delay and price difference of every copy and can skip a trade if price has moved more than you allow.

Can a bot copy signals from a Telegram channel I don't own?

Only by signing in as your own Telegram user account through the Telegram API, because a Telegram bot can only read channels where it has been added as an admin. You set this up with your own API ID, and you must follow Telegram's terms and the channel's own rules. If you run the channel, a normal bot added as an admin is the simpler route.

Do prop firms allow trade copiers?

It depends on the firm, and rules change. Many ban copying trades from other people or across accounts owned by different people, and some allow copying only between your own accounts at the same firm. Read your firm's current rules before we start; we build the limits you ask for, but following the firm's terms is your responsibility.

Will copying a trader make money?

No one can promise that. A copier repeats the master's trades on your accounts, so losses are copied as well as gains, and slippage and fees make results differ from the master's. Start with a demo account, then small size and tight loss limits.
