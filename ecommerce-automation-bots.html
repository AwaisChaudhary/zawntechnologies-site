Ecommerce automation and price monitoring bots

We build bots that keep orders and stock in sync across Shopify, WooCommerce and marketplaces, watch competitor and supplier prices, and alert you on Telegram, Slack or email when something needs a decision.

What an ecommerce bot is for

Most online stores lose hours every week to copying data between systems: orders into the warehouse sheet, stock counts back into the store, supplier price changes into the catalogue. An ecommerce bot does those jobs on a schedule or the moment an event happens, using the platforms' official APIs wherever they exist. It follows rules you set, such as "never price below cost plus 15%", and it asks a person before anything unusual.

What we build

  • Order and inventory sync: one stock count across Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon Seller Central, eBay or Etsy, so an item sold on one channel is reduced everywhere; orders pushed to your warehouse, 3PL or accounting tool.
  • Price monitoring and tracking bots: daily or hourly checks of competitor, supplier or marketplace prices from public pages or APIs, stored with history, with alerts when a price moves past a threshold.
  • Repricing rules: suggested or automatic price updates within a floor and ceiling you set, with a log of every change and a pause switch.
  • Restock and back-in-stock alerts: low-stock warnings to your team and supplier reorder drafts; back-in-stock emails to your own customers who asked for them.
  • Catalogue and feed bots: supplier CSV or XML feeds imported, cleaned and mapped to your products; Google Merchant and marketplace feeds kept current.
  • Customer-service bots: order-status answers on your site, WhatsApp or Messenger, with handover to a person. See AI chatbot development.
  • Telegram and Slack reports: a morning summary of sales, low stock, failed payments and price changes, plus commands such as /stock SKU.

How a price monitoring and sync bot works

  1. SourcesStore APIs and webhooks, supplier feeds, public competitor pages
  2. CollectorScheduled jobs that fetch, retry and respect rate limits
  3. Your rulesPrice floors, stock buffers, which changes need approval
  4. ActionsUpdate stock or price, create an order, draft a reorder
  5. Alerts and logTelegram, Slack or email, with a history of every change

Stock and price changes are written through the store's official API, and every change is logged so it can be checked or undone.

Features we include as standard

  • A simple dashboard or Google Sheet showing what the bot did and what it is watching
  • Approval mode: the bot suggests price changes and a person confirms them, until you trust it
  • Limits: maximum price change per day, a minimum margin and a stock safety buffer
  • Retries and duplicate protection, so an order is never sent to the warehouse twice
  • Alerts when a source page changes layout or an API key expires, instead of silent failure
  • Store and API credentials kept in a secrets store with the narrowest permissions

Shopify bot traffic and bot protection

Store owners also search for the opposite problem: bots hitting their own shop. Fake sessions, card-testing attempts on checkout and scraping can distort analytics and cost money. We can help with practical steps: Shopify's built-in bot protection and checkout settings, rate limits and bot rules at your CDN (for example Cloudflare), hCaptcha or similar on forms, filtering known bots out of reports, and alerts on unusual order patterns. We do not promise that any setup stops every bot.

Amazon price tracking bots with the Keepa API

For Amazon sellers and sourcing teams, the Keepa API is often a better data source than scraping Amazon pages. It returns price history, Buy Box, sales-rank and offer data by ASIN. We build bots that check your ASIN lists against your own rules, such as "alert me when the Buy Box drops below my cost plus fees" or "flag products whose sales rank improved this week", and send the matches to Telegram, Discord, Slack, email or a Google Sheet. Keepa's API is a paid, token-based subscription in your own name, so we size how often the bot checks to fit your token budget. Your own listings, orders and fees come from Amazon's official Selling Partner API.

  • Typical build: ASIN watchlist, rule checks, alerts and a price-history sheet: about 20 to 50 hours ($1,000 to $2,500).
  • With a lead dashboard: sourcing scores, fee and margin estimates from your inputs, and filters: about 50 to 100 hours ($2,500 to $5,000).

Restock monitor bots: alerts, not auto-checkout

A restock monitor watches product pages or a supplier's feed and tells you the moment an item is back in stock or a new product is listed. We build these for legitimate uses: tracking your suppliers and wholesale portals, watching your own store's inventory, and alerting your team or customers who asked to be told. Alerts go to Telegram, Discord, Slack or email, with checks spaced politely and a warning when a page changes layout. We do not add auto-checkout, buying bots, or anything that gets around a store's queue, purchase limits or bot protection.

Tech stack

Python and Node.js; Shopify Admin GraphQL API and webhooks, WooCommerce REST API, Amazon Selling Partner API, the Keepa API, eBay and Etsy APIs; Playwright or HTTP scrapers for public pages where no API exists; PostgreSQL for price history; Redis queues; cron or cloud schedulers; Telegram Bot API and Slack webhooks; Docker on your cloud account. More on data collection is on the web scraping services page.

How we work

  1. Map the process. Which systems, which data, what should happen and what must never happen.
  2. Fixed estimate per milestone. You know the cost before each stage starts.
  3. Build on a test store. A Shopify development store or a WooCommerce staging site first, never your live catalogue.
  4. Run in approval mode. The bot reports what it would change for a week or two before it changes anything itself.
  5. Hand over. Code in your repository, deployment on your account, a short guide and optional monthly support.

Risks, rules and what we do not build

  • You are responsible for the terms of each marketplace, platform and website the bot uses, and for local laws including data protection. We use official APIs wherever they exist and collect only public data from other sites, at a polite rate.
  • Automatic repricing can start a price race with other bots. Floors, daily change limits and approval mode keep that under control.
  • We do not build fake review or fake buyer bots, checkout or sneaker scalper bots, bots that get around a site's purchase limits or bot protection, or bots that buy and resell limited stock.

Questions clients ask

How much does a price monitoring bot cost?

At $50 per hour, a price tracker for up to a few hundred products with Telegram or email alerts is usually 20 to 40 hours ($1,000 to $2,000). Competitor monitoring with repricing rules and a dashboard is about 60 to 120 hours ($3,000 to $6,000). Running costs are often $10 to $50 a month.

Can a bot keep my Shopify and Amazon stock in sync?

Yes. The bot listens for orders on each channel through the official Shopify and Amazon Selling Partner APIs and updates the shared stock count, with a safety buffer so you do not oversell during a delay. A two-channel sync is usually 50 to 100 hours.

Is it legal to monitor competitor prices?

Collecting publicly shown prices is common, but rules depend on each site's terms and your country's laws. We collect only public data at a polite rate, use official APIs or data feeds where they exist, and do not log in to other sellers' accounts or bypass access controls. Check with a lawyer if you are unsure.

Can the bot change my prices automatically?

It can, within a floor and ceiling you set and a maximum change per day. Most clients start in approval mode, where the bot suggests changes in Telegram or a dashboard and a person confirms them.

Can you stop bots from attacking my Shopify store?

We can reduce bot traffic and card-testing with Shopify's own protection settings, CDN bot rules and rate limits, form captchas and alerts on unusual orders. No setup stops every bot, and we say so.

Can you build an Amazon price tracker with the Keepa API?

Yes. The bot checks your ASIN list through the Keepa API against rules you set, such as a Buy Box price below your cost plus fees, and sends matches to Telegram, Discord, email or a Google Sheet. A watchlist with alerts is usually 20 to 50 hours ($1,000 to $2,500). The Keepa subscription is in your name and is paid separately.

Do you build restock monitor bots?

Yes, for alerts: a bot that watches supplier pages, wholesale portals or your own store and tells you when an item is back in stock or newly listed. We do not build auto-checkout or buying bots, or anything that gets around a store's purchase limits or bot protection.

Do you build checkout or sneaker bots?

No. We do not build checkout, sneaker or scalper bots, fake review or fake buyer bots, or anything that gets around a store's purchase limits or bot protection.
