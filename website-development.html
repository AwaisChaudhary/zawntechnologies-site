Home / Services / Website design and development

Business websites that load fast, rank well and bring enquiries

We design and build websites for companies and startups: clear messaging, fast pages, technical SEO and contact flows that make it easy for visitors to become clients.

Why teams hire us for this

A website is a sales tool. We start with who your visitors are and what you want them to do, then design pages around that, instead of starting from a template and filling in words.

Technical SEO, speed and analytics are built in from launch, so the site can grow its search traffic over time.

What we build

  • Company and startup websites
  • Landing pages for campaigns and products
  • Service pages structured for search
  • Blogs and content sections
  • Contact forms, booking and live chat
  • Redesigns and migrations without losing rankings

How we work

Every engagement starts with a short discovery phase: we learn your users, your data and your constraints, then send a written plan with milestones and a fixed estimate per milestone. You see working software every one to two weeks, and the code lives in your repository from day one.

Our rate is $50 per hour. For fixed-scope work we quote per milestone so you know the cost before each stage starts.

Questions clients ask

How long does a website take?

A focused business site usually takes 2 to 5 weeks, depending on the number of pages and how ready the content is.

Which platform will you use?

WordPress if your team wants to edit pages easily, or a custom static site or Next.js for speed and flexibility. We recommend based on how you will use it.

Will it rank on Google?

We build the technical foundations, structure and speed that search engines need. Rankings also depend on content and links over time.
