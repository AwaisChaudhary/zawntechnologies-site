Telegram and Discord bots that run your community and workflows

We build bots that moderate communities, send alerts, take payments, answer questions with AI and connect chat to your own systems.

Why teams hire us for this

A bot is often the fastest way to put a product where your users already spend their time. We design commands and flows people actually use, and we make sure the bot keeps running when your group grows from hundreds to tens of thousands of members.

We handle the parts that break small bots: rate limits, reconnections, database storage, admin controls and logging.

What we build

  • Community moderation, onboarding and role management
  • Price, trading and system alerts
  • Paid memberships and subscriptions with Stripe or crypto payments
  • AI assistants that answer questions from your documents
  • Telegram Mini Apps and Discord activities
  • Integrations with CRMs, spreadsheets, exchanges and internal tools

Hire a Discord bot developer

Discord servers usually need a bot for one of four jobs: onboarding and roles (verification, reaction or button roles, tickets), moderation (spam and link filters, raid protection, logs), member features (levels, polls, events, giveaways run fairly) and integrations (posting from your app, GitHub, a store or a price feed). We build with discord.js or discord.py using slash commands, buttons and modals, register the correct gateway intents, and shard the bot when your server or server count grows. You get the source code, an admin config and hosting on your own account.

  • Typical Discord bot jobs: ticket and support bot (20–40 h), role, verification and onboarding bot (15–35 h), paid-membership roles linked to Stripe or Patreon (30–60 h), multi-server public bot with a web dashboard (100+ h).

Hire a Telegram bot developer

Telegram bots are popular for customer support, paid channels, alerts and small shops inside the chat. We build on the official Bot API with python-telegram-bot, aiogram or grammY: inline keyboards, payments through Telegram's payment providers, Stars or Stripe links, group admin tools, scheduled posts and Telegram Mini Apps for anything that needs a full screen. Webhooks keep the bot fast; PostgreSQL keeps user state safe across restarts.

  • Typical Telegram bot jobs: FAQ and lead bot (8–20 h), paid channel or subscription bot (30–60 h), order or booking bot connected to your system (40–80 h), Mini App with a custom web interface (80–160 h).

Telegram trading bot development

Many traders want to control a bot from their phone. We build Telegram front ends for a trading strategy you define: commands such as /status, /pause and /close, trade and error alerts, daily P&L summaries, and buttons to confirm a signal before an order is placed. The bot talks to the exchange or broker through its official API with keys stored on the server, never inside the chat. The strategy itself is built on our crypto trading bot, trading bot or MT5 Expert Advisor pages. A bot automates your rules; it does not promise profit, and trading carries a real risk of loss. We do not build meme-coin sniper bots. To copy signals posted in a Telegram channel into MT5 or an exchange, see Telegram signal copiers.

  1. Telegram chatYour commands, buttons and alerts
  2. Bot serverChecks who is asking, applies your limits, logs every command
  3. Strategy engineYour rules, risk caps and kill switch
  4. Exchange or broker APIOrders and balances through official APIs

Only your Telegram user IDs can send trading commands, and order size limits are enforced on the server, not in the chat.

Hire a Slack bot developer: Slack apps for teams

Inside a company, the chat tool is often Slack, and a bot there saves people from switching between systems. We build Slack apps with Slack's Bolt framework (JavaScript or Python): slash commands such as /ticket or /report, Block Kit messages with buttons and forms, approval flows (leave, expenses, deploys), alerts from your app, CRM or monitoring, daily stand-up and report bots, and AI assistants that answer from your documents with links to the source. Events arrive over the Events API or Socket Mode, and the app asks only for the OAuth scopes it needs.

  • Typical Slack bot jobs: alert or report bot posting from one system (10–25 h), slash command and approval flow connected to your tools (25–50 h), AI assistant over your docs in Slack (40–80 h), Slack app installed by other companies, with OAuth and a settings page (80–160 h).

One rule to plan for: since 2025 Slack gives much lower limits on reading message history to apps sold to other workspaces outside the Slack Marketplace, while apps built for your own workspace keep the normal limits. If you plan to sell a Slack app, we check Slack's current limits and Marketplace review rules with you before we start. For workflows across many tools, see business automation; for an assistant that takes actions, see AI agent development.

Platform rules and what we do not build

  • You are responsible for following Telegram's and Discord's terms and the laws where you and your members are; we use the official bot APIs, not self-bots or user-account automation.
  • We do not build spam or mass-DM bots, fake member or engagement bots, raid tools, or bots for betting or gambling.

How we work

Every engagement starts with a short discovery phase: we learn your users, your data and your constraints, then send a written plan with milestones and a fixed estimate per milestone. You see working software every one to two weeks, and the code lives in your repository from day one.

Our rate is $50 per hour. For fixed-scope work we quote per milestone so you know the cost before each stage starts.

Questions clients ask

How much does a Slack bot cost to build?

At $50 per hour, a bot that posts alerts or reports from one system is usually 10 to 25 hours ($500 to $1,250). A slash command and approval flow connected to your tools is about 25 to 50 hours ($1,250 to $2,500), and an AI assistant that answers from your documents in Slack is about 40 to 80 hours ($2,000 to $4,000).

Does a Slack bot need approval from our Slack admin?

Usually, yes. A Slack app asks for permission scopes, such as reading a channel or posting messages, and many workspaces let only admins approve new apps. We list every scope and why it is needed, ask for the fewest possible, and can build the app for your workspace only so it never appears in the Slack Marketplace.

How long does a bot take?

A focused bot usually takes 1 to 3 weeks. Bots with payments, dashboards or AI take longer and are quoted per milestone.

Can one bot work on Telegram and Discord?

Yes. We can share the core logic and build a thin layer for each platform.

Where is the bot hosted?

On your own cloud account or a server we set up for you, with monitoring and automatic restarts.

How much does it cost to hire a Discord bot developer?

At $50 per hour, a ticket, role or moderation bot is usually 15 to 40 hours ($750 to $2,000). A paid-membership bot is about 30 to 60 hours, and a public multi-server bot with a web dashboard is 100 hours or more.

Can you build a Telegram trading bot?

Yes, as a control and alert layer for a strategy you define: commands to check status, pause or close positions, alerts for trades and errors, and confirmation buttons. Orders go through the exchange or broker's official API. We do not promise profits, and we do not build meme-coin sniper bots.

Can a Telegram bot take payments?

Yes. Telegram supports payments through its listed payment providers and Telegram Stars for digital goods, and a bot can also send Stripe checkout links and manage access to paid channels after payment.
