iOS and Android apps, from first version to the hundredth release

We design, build and publish mobile apps for startups and established businesses, then keep them running as operating systems and users change.

Why teams hire us for this

We have been shipping mobile apps since 2014, from children's learning apps to field tools used on job sites. We choose native Swift and Kotlin when performance or device features matter most, and Flutter or React Native when one codebase for both platforms is the better trade.

Every project includes store submission, crash reporting and analytics, so you know how the app is used from day one.

What we build

  • MVPs for startups that need to test an idea quickly
  • Field and operations apps with offline mode and sync
  • Consumer apps with subscriptions and in-app purchases
  • Apps with camera, maps, Bluetooth and sensor features
  • Rebuilds and rescues of existing apps that have stalled
  • App Store and Google Play submission and review handling

How we work

Every engagement starts with a short discovery phase: we learn your users, your data and your constraints, then send a written plan with milestones and a fixed estimate per milestone. You see working software every one to two weeks, and the code lives in your repository from day one.

Our rate is $50 per hour. For fixed-scope work we quote per milestone so you know the cost before each stage starts.

Questions clients ask

Native or cross-platform?

Cross-platform (Flutter or React Native) suits most business apps and saves 30 to 40 percent of build time. We recommend native when the app depends on heavy graphics, real-time camera work or the newest platform features.

How much does an app cost?

Our rate is $50 per hour. A focused MVP is often 250 to 450 hours. Read our cost guide for a fuller breakdown, or send us your idea for an estimate.

Do you help after launch?

Yes. Most clients keep us on a monthly plan for updates, OS upgrades and new features.
