How to hire a remote software development team in Pakistan

By Awais Chaudhry, 18 November 2026

Pakistan has a large, experienced software industry and a long track record of working with clients in the US, UK, Europe and Australia. Hiring a team here can give you strong engineering at a lower cost than local agencies, but only if you choose carefully. This guide explains how to do that, from a team that has worked with international clients from Lahore since 2014.

Why companies hire remote teams in Pakistan

  • Cost. Experienced teams typically charge well below US or UK agency rates. Our own rate is $50 per hour, and we quote fixed prices per milestone.
  • English. English is widely used in business and universities, so written specifications and calls work without translation.
  • Range of skills. Mobile, web, AI, computer vision, Unity, IoT and blockchain teams are all available, often under one roof.
  • Working hours. Many teams deliberately work evening and night shifts in Pakistan so they overlap with the US East Coast and UK working day.

Freelancer, agency or in-house hire?

A single freelancer suits a small, well-defined task. An agency or established team suits a product that needs several skills (design, mobile, backend, testing) and continuity if one person is unavailable. Hiring employees directly in Pakistan is possible but needs a local entity or an employer-of-record service, which rarely makes sense for a single project.

How to check a team before you hire

Look for verified history, not a polished website

Anyone can write a good website. Look for reviews on a platform where only paying clients can leave them, and read the detailed ones. On Freelancer.com, for example, a profile shows the number of reviews, the share of projects delivered on time and on budget, and badges such as Preferred Freelancer that the platform awards based on performance.

Ask about a project like yours

Ask the team to walk you through a similar project: what was hard, what they would do differently, and what the client said at the end. Specific answers are a good sign. Vague answers about "cutting-edge solutions" are not.

Start with a paid first milestone

Instead of a free test, pay for a small, real first milestone such as a clickable prototype or one working feature. You will learn more about communication, code quality and honesty about problems than any interview can show.

Set the project up so you stay in control

  • Code in your repository from day one. Create the GitHub or GitLab repository yourself and invite the team.
  • Accounts in your name. App store, cloud hosting, domain and third-party services should be registered to your company.
  • Milestones with demos. Pay per milestone, and only after you have seen and tested the working software.
  • Written scope. Agree what each milestone includes, and just as importantly, what it does not.
  • One main contact. Know who answers your questions and who is accountable for deadlines.

Time zones and communication

Pakistan Standard Time is UTC+5 all year. When it is 9 AM in New York in winter, it is 7 PM in Lahore, so teams that work evening shifts can join your morning meetings. With the UK the overlap is easier: a UK afternoon is a Pakistani evening. Agree on a fixed weekly call, written progress updates, and a shared place for questions so nothing depends on memory.

Contracts and payments

Freelance platforms handle contracts and hold milestone payments, which protects both sides; that is how most of our clients have worked with us. For direct contracts, agree in writing on scope, milestones, intellectual property transfer on payment, confidentiality, and how changes to scope are priced. International bank transfers, Payoneer and Wise are the usual payment routes.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing on the lowest hourly rate. A team that needs twice the hours is not cheaper.
  • Skipping the written scope because the first call went well.
  • Letting the team own the accounts or the code repository.
  • Paying large amounts up front instead of per milestone.
  • Going silent for weeks and expecting the right result at the end.

Questions clients ask

Is it safe to hire developers from Pakistan?

Yes, if you check the team the same way you would anywhere: verified reviews, a paid trial milestone, code in your own repository, and payment by milestone. Platforms such as Freelancer.com and Upwork add payment protection on top.

What time zone is Pakistan in?

Pakistan Standard Time is UTC+5 all year, with no daylight saving. Many Pakistani teams, including ours, work evening and night shifts so their hours overlap with the US East Coast and the UK.

How do I pay a software team in Pakistan?

The simplest routes are a freelance platform with milestone payments, an international bank transfer, or services such as Payoneer or Wise. Agree the currency, invoice format and milestone schedule in writing before work starts.
