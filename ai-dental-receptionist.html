An AI receptionist that answers every call to your dental practice

Patients call when the front desk is busy, at lunch or after hours. Our AI receptionist answers in a natural voice, books and reschedules appointments, answers insurance and opening-hours questions, and hands urgent calls to your team.

Get early access

The problem

Every missed call to a dental practice can be a missed new patient. The front desk is often on another line, checking a patient in, or closed. A human answering service costs hundreds a month and cannot book into your schedule, and a full-time receptionist costs far more.

What it does

  • Answers 24/7. Picks up every call in a natural voice, including evenings, weekends and lunch breaks
  • Books appointments. Offers real open slots, books, reschedules and cancels, and confirms by text
  • Missed-call text back. If a caller hangs up, they get a text within a minute with a booking link
  • Knows your practice. Answers questions on hours, location, services, accepted insurance and new-patient forms from your own information
  • Urgent calls go to people. Dental emergencies and anything it should not handle are transferred to your team or flagged straight away
  • Call summaries. Every call is transcribed and summarised, so your team sees what happened at a glance

Questions

Will patients know it is an AI?

It introduces itself as the practice's virtual assistant. Most callers just want an appointment booked quickly, and anyone can ask for a person at any time.

Does it work with our practice software?

Our first versions connect to Google Calendar and common online booking tools; tell us which practice management system you use when you join early access so we can prioritise it.

What about patient privacy?

Calls are handled over encrypted connections and we only store what is needed to book and summarise. We will share our data handling details with every early-access practice before going live.

Do we keep our phone number?

Yes. Your existing number forwards to the assistant when your team does not answer, or all the time if you prefer.

When can we start?

We are onboarding practices in small groups. Join early access and we will contact you when your spot is ready, with the founding price locked in.
