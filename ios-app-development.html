iOS apps for iPhone and iPad, built natively and launched properly

We design and build native iOS apps in Swift and SwiftUI, get them through App Store review, and keep them updated as Apple releases new iOS versions.

Why teams hire us for this

Native iOS development gives you the best performance and full access to Apple features such as the camera, Apple Pay, HealthKit, widgets and on-device machine learning. We have been certified in iPhone development and shipping mobile apps for over a decade.

We plan for App Store review from day one: privacy labels, permissions, in-app purchase rules and the details that commonly cause rejections.

What we build

  • Native iPhone and iPad apps in Swift and SwiftUI
  • Camera, AR and Core ML features
  • Apple Pay, in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • HealthKit, widgets and Apple Watch companions
  • App Store submission and review handling
  • Updates for new iOS versions and devices

How we work

Every engagement starts with a short discovery phase: we learn your users, your data and your constraints, then send a written plan with milestones and a fixed estimate per milestone. You see working software every one to two weeks, and the code lives in your repository from day one.

Our rate is $50 per hour. For fixed-scope work we quote per milestone so you know the cost before each stage starts.

Questions clients ask

Native iOS or cross-platform?

If you also need Android and your app is mostly screens and data, cross-platform is usually cheaper. Choose native when you need peak performance or deep Apple features.

Do you handle App Store submission?

Yes, including screenshots, privacy details and any questions from Apple's reviewers.

Can you take over an existing iOS app?

Yes. We start with a code review and a list of risks before making changes.
