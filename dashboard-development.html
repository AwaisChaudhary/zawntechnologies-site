Dashboards that put your business numbers in one place

We connect your sales, finance, operations and product data and build live dashboards in Power BI, Looker Studio or a custom web app.

Why teams hire us for this

Most reporting pain is a data problem, not a chart problem: numbers live in five tools and nobody trusts which one is right. We start by pulling data into one clean place and agreeing definitions, then build the dashboards.

When off-the-shelf BI tools are too limited or too expensive per user, we build custom dashboards into your own product or portal.

What we build

  • Power BI and Looker Studio dashboards
  • Data pipelines from CRMs, ads, e-commerce and accounting tools
  • Custom web dashboards and client portals
  • IoT and sensor monitoring dashboards
  • Automated scheduled reports by email
  • KPI definitions and data cleaning

How we work

Every engagement starts with a short discovery phase: we learn your users, your data and your constraints, then send a written plan with milestones and a fixed estimate per milestone. You see working software every one to two weeks, and the code lives in your repository from day one.

Our rate is $50 per hour. For fixed-scope work we quote per milestone so you know the cost before each stage starts.

Questions clients ask

Power BI or a custom dashboard?

Power BI or Looker Studio is usually faster and cheaper for internal reporting. Custom dashboards make sense for client-facing portals or when per-user licences add up.

Can you combine data from many tools?

Yes. We connect APIs, databases and spreadsheets into one data store, refreshed on a schedule.

How live is the data?

From daily refreshes to near real time, depending on the sources and your budget.
